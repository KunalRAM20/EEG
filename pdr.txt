# Project Definition Report (PDR)

## Project Summary

This project proposes the development of an Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML)-based Clinical Decision Support System for epileptic seizure prediction, severity classification, and doctor-assisted decision making. Epilepsy is a neurological disorder that affects millions of people worldwide, and diagnosing and assessing seizure severity requires neurologists to analyse EEG recordings, medical reports, imaging results, laboratory findings, medication history, and other patient information before making treatment decisions. Existing machine learning research primarily focuses on binary seizure detection using EEG signals and provides prediction results that are often difficult for clinicians to interpret in routine practice. Most existing systems do not combine multiple patient data sources, provide clinically meaningful severity grading, generate readable medical reports, or allow doctors to validate AI-generated recommendations. This research aims to address these limitations by developing an end-to-end clinical support system that integrates multi-modal patient data, including EEG, ECG, MRI and X-ray reports, laboratory results, medical history, medication history, and clinician notes, to produce accurate seizure risk predictions and clinically useful outputs. The collected data will undergo preprocessing, including signal filtering, normalization, segmentation, and feature extraction, before being analysed using a range of machine learning and deep learning models such as Random Forest, Support Vector Machine (SVM), K-Nearest Neighbour (KNN), Gradient Boosting, CNN, LSTM, and CNN-LSTM. The best-performing model will be selected to predict seizure risk and classify patients into five clinically meaningful severity levels ranging from Level 1 (Mild) to Level 5 (Critical). Unlike traditional seizure detection systems, this project introduces a clinically oriented severity grading framework designed to assist doctors in understanding patient condition more effectively and prioritising treatment decisions. Following prediction, an AI-based report generation module will automatically produce a structured, easy-to-read clinical summary containing seizure risk, severity level, important findings, supporting evidence, and possible treatment recommendations. Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) techniques, including SHAP and LIME, will be incorporated to explain the reasoning behind each prediction, thereby improving transparency, reliability, and clinician trust in the system. The proposed framework also includes a doctor validation workflow in which clinicians review, modify, and approve AI-generated reports before making final clinical decisions, ensuring that the system functions as a decision support tool rather than replacing medical expertise. The final outcome of this research will be a web-based clinical application that enables patient data upload, automated seizure severity prediction, AI-assisted report generation, explainable prediction analysis, and doctor validation within a single integrated platform. By combining machine learning, explainable AI, and clinical workflow support, this research aims to reduce the time required for patient assessment, improve the quality of clinical decision making, and bridge the gap between current machine learning research and practical healthcare applications while ensuring that the final responsibility for diagnosis and treatment remains with qualified medical professionals.
